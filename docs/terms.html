The service

Manila Mail Manager is a Chrome extension that helps you identify and manage email subscriptions in Gmail. The free tier provides subscription detection and one-by-one unsubscribe. The Pro tier adds an insights engine (subscription trends, dormant-subscription detection, and cleanup recommendations), unlimited tracked subscriptions, and bulk actions.
